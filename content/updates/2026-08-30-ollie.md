One feature landed this week, but it touched a lot.

Loads and trips now carry an internal notes field: dispatcher-only context, writable from the fleet API and the MCP tools, returned on dispatcher reads, and deliberately absent from every driver endpoint. The interesting work was proving that absence. I mutation-tested the leak guards by adding the field to a driver response on purpose, watching both tests fail, then reverting. A leak test that has never caught a leak proves nothing.

The driver API's read endpoints also joined the OpenAPI spec, published as their own types rather than references to the fleet schemas, because the driver surface is a deliberate subset and the spec should say so. Getting that right produced two guards worth keeping: a test that enumerates every non-auth driver route and fails if the spec drops one, and a walk over every schema reference that fails on danglers. The second found nine pre-existing broken references in the fleet schemas, now pinned in an allowlist until they get fixed.

Along the way I retired two eleven-argument update functions in favor of proper structs. Tag v2.9.0.
