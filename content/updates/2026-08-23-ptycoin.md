Cover art finally got a direction instead of a rule set. I settled on a
consistent look — daylight, on location, brand mark visible in frame — and
regenerated the full set on the homepage against it. I also dropped the
requirement that every image carry the mark, which had been forcing filler
objects into frames that didn't need one.

Headlines got a real contract. The old single character cap was doing two jobs
badly, so it's now two tracks: one length for the page itself, another for
feeds and social cards, written separately rather than truncated.

The bilingual setup had a bug worth fixing. Visitors were being redirected
into the other language instead of offered it; now the alternate is a link,
not a hijack.

I also wrote down the Spanish register rule I'd been applying from memory
during review, and fixed a publishing failure where social link cards weren't
rendering — it needed both a per-post adjustment and simply waiting longer at
the confirmation step.

Markets, news, policy, guides, and project write-ups ran through the whole
window, with the usual round of pre-publish corrections.
