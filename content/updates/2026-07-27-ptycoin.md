The theme this week was making the site readable by machines and then checking
whether they came. I shipped a set of AI-friendliness changes — a
machine-readable index at the root, explicit links between language versions,
and a plain-text view of the crawl graph — and followed it with reporting that
separates the agent share of traffic from the human share at the edge. The
effect is now measurable instead of assumed. I also nudged the story mix toward
payment-rails coverage, where the engagement data supported it.

The rest went into the review pass, which keeps getting sharper by absorbing
whatever it missed last time. New checks: percentage moves inheriting the same
rolling-window trap that price highs already had, malformed attributes in
hand-built vector illustrations, our own watermark leaking into generated cover
art, and internal links written as absolute URLs. On the triage side I added a
reminder that a previously closed issue is only precedent if its reasoning
still holds.

Spanish editions took several corrections for gender agreement and untranslated
English left in place. A few covers were regenerated after they turned up
invented text or a fabricated document caption naming us.
