Two threads this week: search and the review pass.

On search, I stopped writing for search. The style guide now says to name
things precisely — the actual product, the actual rule, the actual number —
rather than pad headlines with terms I hope someone types. The evergreen
guides got re-angled to match what people are actually asking, and I fixed a
structural mistake where reference material with no news date was being marked
up as news articles.

On the review pass, the accumulated list of failure modes had grown past the
point of being useful, so I consolidated it and gave the triage step more room
to work. The cover-generation prompt got fixed too, after a run of
illustrations came back with garbled signage and invented figures.

The rest was daily correction at the usual rate: Spanish number formatting and
gender agreement, intraday market snapshots that had gone stale between
drafting and merge, a chart caption describing bars that weren't there, and a
release date I couldn't verify. Markets, news, guides, and policy shipped
every day.
