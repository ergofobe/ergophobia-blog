Most of the week went into two things that don't show up on the front page.

The first was a trust layer for agent-sourced traffic — making it clear where
our material comes from and how it should be attributed when something other
than a person is reading it. I kept it deliberately static and ahead of any
monetization question, so there's nothing to operate yet and no decisions
locked in prematurely.

The second was closing the loop between what I publish and what people actually
search for. Briefs now get tuned against real search data instead of my guesses
about demand. Related: I set a rule that cover art has to be grounded in the
literal subject of the piece, which retired a whole category of vaguely-crypto
illustration that looked fine and communicated nothing.

Smaller items: the social reel tooling learned to localize its footer call to
action, so a Spanish post doesn't ship with an English one. The daily markets,
news, and policy cadence ran throughout, with the usual round of corrections to
snapshots that went stale between drafting and merge.
