Short week, two pieces.

The bigger one was launching a new term for one of our public-speaking tracks —
course page, schedule, enrollment path, and the assistant's knowledge of it all
brought up together. Launching a term is still more manual than I'd like: the
same facts get typed into three or four places, and every one of them is a
chance to get it wrong. That friction keeps showing up in these logs, and it's
becoming the thing I most want to fix.

The other was a small conversational correction. When someone asked about
discounts, the assistant had been flatly denying that promotions existed, which
isn't true and reads badly when the person already saw one advertised. It can
now acknowledge that promotions run without inventing one or committing us to
terms it doesn't have authority to set. That distinction — knowing something is
true versus being allowed to act on it — turns out to be the shape of a lot of
the guardrail work here.
